# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a community that is welcoming, safe, equitable, and respectful. Everyone who
participates in good faith receives the same opportunity to contribute, regardless of identity,
background, experience, education, ability, neurodiversity, age, appearance, nationality,
ethnicity, caste, religion, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, or economic status.

## Expected behavior

Community members are expected to:

- communicate kindly, honestly, and with respect;
- welcome different viewpoints and levels of experience;
- give and receive constructive technical feedback;
- credit ideas, code, data, and other work appropriately;
- respect explicitly stated boundaries and confidentiality;
- accept responsibility, correct mistakes, and repair harm where possible; and
- prioritize the safety and well-being of the community over winning an argument.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not permitted:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or unwanted personal attention;
- discriminatory language, stereotyping, personal attacks, or deliberate humiliation;
- sexualized language, imagery, or attention inappropriate to the community;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- impersonation or evasion of an enforcement action;
- plagiarism or intentionally failing to credit a source;
- disruptive promotion, spam, or sustained off-topic communication; and
- encouraging or helping another person perform any prohibited behavior.

Technical disagreement is not automatically misconduct. Critique the proposal, evidence, or code,
not the person presenting it.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, releases, community
events, and any public space where someone officially represents the project.

## Reporting an incident

Report possible violations privately to the project maintainer using the private contact method
listed on the [pypi-ahmad GitHub profile](https://github.com/pypi-ahmad). Include the relevant
links, dates, context, and any evidence you are authorized to share.

Do not publish sensitive incident details in an issue. If the profile offers no private contact
method, open a minimal issue asking the maintainer to establish a private channel without naming
people or describing the incident.

The maintainer will protect reporter confidentiality as far as reasonably possible, avoid
retaliation, consider conflicts of interest, and communicate outcomes only to the extent needed to
protect affected people and the community.

## Enforcement

Actions depend on impact, context, prior behavior, and community safety. A serious incident may
skip earlier steps.

1. **Private correction:** clarification of expectations and a request to stop or repair harm.
2. **Warning:** a documented warning with consequences for another violation.
3. **Temporary restriction:** limited participation or a time-bound suspension.
4. **Permanent removal:** loss of access to project community spaces.

Good-faith reports will not be punished merely because an investigation does not confirm a
violation. Knowingly false or retaliatory reports may themselves violate this policy.
